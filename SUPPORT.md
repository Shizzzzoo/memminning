# Getting support

Start with [installation](docs/INSTALLATION.md), [compatibility](docs/COMPATIBILITY.md) and [troubleshooting](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

- **Bugs:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/Shizzzzoo/memminning/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature/device requests:** [request a feature](https://github.com/Shizzzzoo/memminning/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Usage questions:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Shizzzzoo/memminning/issues/new) with the question and environment.
- **Security-sensitive issues:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Community support is best effort; there is no response-time guarantee or commercial support contract. Version 1 is a public preview, with Arch/GNOME as the primary tested environment.

Include the app version, architecture, distribution, kernel, desktop version, Wayland/X11, expected/actual behavior, reproduction steps and whether recovery succeeded. Attach only a reviewed/redacted diagnostic export or relevant log excerpt. Never share credentials, unrelated private files or full environment dumps.

For an incomplete session, keep `session.json` and try `~/.local/bin/memminning --restore`. Do not uninstall or erase the recovery journal just to clear an error.
